# Litefuse licensing: what comes from where

## 1. The community core — a fork of Langfuse (MIT)

Litefuse is a fork of Langfuse (https://github.com/langfuse/langfuse), including the
evaluator work in this repository. The community core is taken from the MIT-licensed
Langfuse core and stays MIT-licensed here. The upstream copyright notice
(`Copyright (c) 2023-2025 Langfuse GmbH`) is preserved in `/LICENSE`, as the MIT license
requires, and `/LICENSE` states the fork explicitly.

## 2. The enterprise edition — Litefuse's own work

The paths listed in ANNEX A of `LICENSE-EE` are Litefuse's own work: the enterprise
implementation of this product, licensed to customers under the Litefuse Enterprise
License.

## 3. How the split is decided

ANNEX A (enterprise) and ANNEX B (community, MIT) are Litefuse's own product split. They
are scoped by what Litefuse sells and what it gives away, and by which paths the community
edition needs in order to run on its own. The list is maintained by Litefuse for its own
product purposes and does not follow a directory rule of another project.

## 4. Third-party components

Third-party components incorporated into this software keep the licence their owner
granted. `/LICENSE` says so, and neither file above changes those licences.
